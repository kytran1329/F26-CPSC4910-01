Our Product

Road Reward connects trucking companies, drivers, and incentive rewards in one convenient web application. Sponsors can manage their participating drivers, award or deduct points based on driving performance, and maintain a catalog of available rewards. Drivers can view their point balance, browse available products, and use their earned points to make purchases.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide an easy-to-use incentive system that encourages drivers to improve their on-road performance while giving sponsors the tools they need to manage driver participation, rewards, and reporting.
